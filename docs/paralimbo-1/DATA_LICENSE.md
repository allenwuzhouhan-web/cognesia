**Experimental authors:** Eyal Gruntman, Sandro Romani and Michael B. Reiser.

**Paper:** “Simple integration of fast excitation and offset, delayed inhibition computes directional selectivity in Drosophila.” *Nature Neuroscience* 21, 250–257 (2018). [DOI 10.1038/s41593-017-0046-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-017-0046-4).

**Changes:** conversion of source recordings, acquisition filtering, baseline subtraction, selection of one matching trial for each of nine conditions, endpoint calculation, pairing with model predictions and plotting. Interactive display traces are rounded to 1e-9 mV; `results.json` retains the scored trace values. These are derived research outputs rather than an unchanged redistribution of the complete original archive.
