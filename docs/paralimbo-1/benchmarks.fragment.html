Study limits and failed requests
  • One recorded cell, one date group, one experimental dataset; five related endpoints are not five independent experiments.
  • Acquisition RF center and axis were experimentally selected; no independently measured BANC correspondence.
  • Right hemisphere mirrors the left-eye recording; original wiring is retained for every arm.
  • Recorded traces retain legacy acquisition-origin resample_poly plus onset-grid interpolation. Predictions use a centered FIR on physical output times; unavailable acquisition phase makes these approximate, not identical observation operators.
  • The three-sigma circular RF cutoff is an additional frozen implementation assumption beyond the donor finite-arena blur; both providers and numerical refinements use identical support.
  • The fixed boundary is not an optimized strong BANC baseline. Gain uncertainty is not exhausted.
  • Biological superiority is unestablished; any win is conditional on this retrospective boundary and cell assignment.
  • Single selected recording trial per condition; no repeat averaging, SEM, confidence interval or replication claim.
  • Only the assumed T4c assignment is executed. T4d and sensory gain sensitivity remain unexecuted.
  • Every request undergoes actual complete-state disk checkpoint restore and exact replay. Campaign checkpoint files are temporary and removed after checking; retained digests and selected-root traces do not permit offline full-state replay.
  • Biological superiority: not established. Five related endpoint views share one recorded cell and dataset.
  • No failed native requests in the sealed inventory.
