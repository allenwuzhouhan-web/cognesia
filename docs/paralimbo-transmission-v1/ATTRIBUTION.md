# Attribution and distribution

**Biological validation: PENDING.** This experimental release reports an
inconclusive retrospective comparison. Attribution does not imply endorsement.

The recorded T4 voltage traces and processed observation exports are adapted
from Gruntman, Romani and Reiser (2018), *Simple integration of fast excitation
and offset, delayed inhibition computes directional selectivity in Drosophila*,
[Nature Neuroscience](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-017-0046-4).
The source dataset is [Gruntman_2017_Data.zip, version 1](https://doi.org/10.25378/janelia.5576101.v1),
under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

Changes: one recorded T4 cell and nine conditions were selected; voltage samples
were converted/filtered, baselines subtracted and onset grids aligned. Each
condition is scored against the minimum-index eligible trial, without repeat
averaging or confidence intervals. Observation NPZs retain the eligible trials
and associated summaries for auditing this selection. These derived recordings
retain CC BY-NC 4.0. Cognesia's software permissions do not replace their terms.

personal-use permissions described in [LICENSE](LICENSE). The software is not

BANC anatomy is credited to Bates et al. and The BANC-FlyWire Consortium;
FlyWire annotations retain their original provider attribution. This release
contains diagnostic summaries and source references, not compiled connectomes,

Those inputs require separate acquisition under their providers' terms.

The primary descending-population dataset was considered at
[DOI 10.7910/DVN/INYAYV](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/INYAYV), version 1.0.
