COGNESIA Account privacy

Your account information.

Cognesia uses Google Firebase Authentication to verify your email address and manage email or Google sign-in. Phone signup is currently unavailable. Verification messages are transactional, not marketing.

The account service stores an account identifier, the dates and status of your access keys, and a one-way verifier for each key. It does not store the original access-key secret. Your verified contact information is held by the authentication provider and shown only in your signed-in account.

Your account session is held in browser memory and is cleared when the page closes. The key displayed after creation is not saved to browser storage. Copying a key places it on your device’s clipboard.

Netlify hosts the account website and API. Authentication requests are sent to Google and the Cognesia account service over HTTPS. Netlify supplies an approximate country based on your connection address; the service uses it to restrict account access in mainland China, Cuba, Laos, North Korea and Vietnam. Unknown connection locations are also denied. This is not GPS tracking, and VPNs or proxies can affect the detected location. Service providers may keep operational and abuse-prevention logs. There are no advertising or analytics scripts in this account page.

Account sign-in does not upload local neural data, recordings, research chats or simulations. Keys authorize local app access and can be revoked or replaced from your account.

For account help or deletion, contact the project owner through their public GitHub profile. Do not post passwords, keys or phone numbers in public issues.
