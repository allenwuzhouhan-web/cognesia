COGNESIA Account privacy

Your account information.

Cognesia uses Google Firebase Authentication to verify your email address or phone number and manage account sign-in. Google processes phone numbers for verification and abuse prevention. Verification messages are transactional, not marketing.

The account service stores an account identifier, the dates and status of your access keys, and a one-way verifier for each key. It does not store the original access-key secret. Your email or phone number is held by the authentication provider and shown only in your signed-in account.

Your account session is held in browser memory and is cleared when the page closes. The key displayed after creation is not saved to browser storage. Copying a key places it on your device’s clipboard.

Authentication requests are sent to Google and the Cognesia account service over HTTPS. Service providers may keep operational and abuse-prevention logs. There are no advertising or analytics scripts in this account page.

Account sign-in does not upload local neural data, recordings, research chats or simulations. Keys authorize local app access and can be revoked or replaced from your account.

For account help or deletion, contact the project owner through their public GitHub profile. Do not post passwords, keys or phone numbers in public issues.
